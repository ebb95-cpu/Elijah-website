  • Comment Bot only reads and replies to comments on my own YouTube channel.

Comment Bot and YouTube

Comment Bot is a tool I use to manage comments on my own YouTube channel. It connects to YouTube through Google's official YouTube Data API, with my permission, using Google sign-in (OAuth). It is not offered to the public, and people who comment on my videos never sign in to it.

What it uses Google and YouTube data for: only to read comments on videos on my channel and to post my replies to them. Nothing else.

What data it accesses:

  • The public text of comments left on my videos
  • The public handle or display name of each commenter
  • Comment IDs and video IDs, so a reply goes to the right comment
  • The replies I write and post
  • A reply log, so the same comment never gets two replies

It does not access commenters' email addresses, private messages, passwords, or any other part of their Google accounts. It does not access my Gmail, contacts, or files.

How it is stored and kept: Comment Bot keeps a reply log so it never replies to the same comment twice. The log holds the comment and video IDs, the commenter's handle, the comment text, and the reply that was posted. It's stored on a private server that only authorized people can access. This data is kept for as long as it's needed to manage replies on my channel. It's deleted when I stop using Comment Bot, or sooner if you ask me to remove it by emailing crushedcrib19@gmail.com. The access credential Google issues is stored securely, is only used to call the YouTube API for my channel, and is never shared. Data is not used to build profiles of commenters and is not combined with other data about them.

Revoking access: the account owner can remove Comment Bot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. If you commented on one of my videos and want your information removed from the reply log, email me and I'll delete it. You can also delete your comment on YouTube at any time.

Google API Limited Use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice, that means Google user data is used only to provide the comment-reply feature described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone except as needed to provide that feature or to comply with the law, and not read by people except where needed for security, legal reasons, or with permission.

Google's own handling of YouTube data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy, and use of YouTube is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service.

This website

elijahbryant.pro is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, map code from unpkg, book covers from Open Library, and video thumbnails from YouTube. When those load, your browser connects to those services, which may see your IP address.

Contact form: the contact form doesn't send anything to this website. It opens a draft in your own email app. If you choose to send it, your name, company, email, and message go to my contact inbox so I can reply. Please don't include sensitive personal information.

Newsletter and waitlist

When you join the Consistency Club newsletter or the Ask Elijah waitlist, your email address is sent to Beehiiv, the service that runs my newsletter. For waitlist signups, Beehiiv also records that you joined the Ask Elijah waitlist and, if your link included them, the campaign tags (UTM parameters) showing where you came from.

Your email is used only to send you the newsletter or Ask Elijah launch news you signed up for. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Beehiiv's handling of your data is covered by the Beehiiv Privacy Policy.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools.

  • Local storage: the homepage saves a short list in your browser of which background video clips you've already seen, so the next visit shows different ones. It contains no personal information, never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.
  • Private access cookie: one cookie is used only for my own private preview access to the paused Ask Elijah beta. Regular visitors never get it.

Beehiiv pages you visit through links on this site have their own cookie practices, described in Beehiiv's policy.

Selling and sharing

I don't sell, rent, or trade personal information, and I don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information is only shared with the services named on this page, to do the job described, or when required by law.

Your choices

  • Ask me to delete or tell you what information I hold about you by contacting me below. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law (such as the GDPR or California law), and I'll honour those requests.

Children

Many young players follow my content, but this website and my tools are not designed to collect personal information from children under 13, and I don't knowingly do so. Parents or guardians should sign up for the newsletter or waitlist on behalf of younger players. If you believe a child under 13 has given me personal information, contact me and I'll delete it.
