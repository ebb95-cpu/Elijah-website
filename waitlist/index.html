What it feels like

i play good in practice but i choke in games when it matters. how do i stop being scared to mess up?

Being scared to mess up means you care. That's not the problem. The problem is when the fear makes you play not to lose.

I went undrafted out of BYU. I got cut. I went to Israel and had to earn everything. On the Bucks I'd go in for a few minutes in the playoffs. When you only get four minutes, you can't play scared. You play the next possession.

So stop playing the whole game in your head. Pick one thing you control: sprint back on D, box out, talk. Do that on the first possession and your body calms down. Mistakes will happen. Next play. Before every game I take a breath and remind myself who I play for. Then I go.

Today's rep: In your next practice, every time you mess up, clap once, say "next play," and sprint to your spot. Count how many times you do it.
