A FIRST LOOK

Start With These Five.

  1. 01

    How many players at my level do you represent, and how much time will you have for me?

  2. 02

    What is my realistic market range now, and what could it be after a strong second season?

  3. 03

    How is your fee calculated, and who pays it in each league or deal we are considering?

  4. 04

    Can I speak privately with two current clients and one former client?

  5. 05

    What are the term, exclusivity rules, and exit process in our agency agreement?

KEEP THE FINAL SAY

Your Agent Works for You.

Listen to the advice. Verify the network. Talk to players who have actually been there. Then make your own decision.
