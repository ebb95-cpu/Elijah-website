A FIRST LOOK

START WITH THESE FIVE.

  1. 01

    Is the salary gross or net, in what currency, and what amount actually reaches your account?

  2. 02

    Who is responsible for local taxes and the agent fee in this specific deal?

  3. 03

    Is your salary guaranteed, and what happens if a payment is late or you are released?

  4. 04

    Are flights, housing, insurance, and family visas written into the contract?

  5. 05

    What are your buyout, termination, and dispute-resolution options?

Every contract and country is different. Have an independent lawyer and a qualified tax professional review your specific terms.

KEEP THE FINAL SAY

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE SIGNING.

A good deal is not just the headline salary. Know what is guaranteed, when money arrives, what your family receives, and how you can leave.
